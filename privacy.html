Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Postiz local is a self-hosted tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It has no public users.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, the app receives an OAuth token from Google with the YouTube scopes needed to upload videos, set their details, and read their analytics, plus the basic profile (name, email) of the signed-in account.

How the data is used and stored

  • Tokens and channel details are stored only in a database on the owner's own computer.
  • They are used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to read the owner's channel analytics.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Postiz local's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting a channel in the app deletes its stored token.
